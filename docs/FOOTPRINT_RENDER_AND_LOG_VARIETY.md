Traffic-only updates now transfer contiguous runs of occupied old/new car or log cells via lcd.show_rect, rather than full 240px rows. The framebuffer still redraws complete changed rows so road markings, water and continuous logs remain consistent. Camera scroll, player movement and overlays still use full redraw. Log lanes use a deterministic per-row stagger in [-1,0,1], and independently seeded speed/direction; no more forced synchronous section movement. This does not prove every generated river is reachable. Validate the full suite and reachability scan before installation; test especially top HUD, log end caps and input latency.
